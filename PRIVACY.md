# Trackr — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 4 July 2026**

Trackr is a personal event-logging app. **The short version: Trackr keeps all your data on your device. It never sends your data anywhere or shares it with anyone.**

## What data Trackr handles

Everything in Trackr is created by you: the events you log (values, notes, timestamps), the categories you define, any photos you attach, and your app settings. All of it is stored **locally on your device**, in the app's private storage. Trackr has no user accounts and no server.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, except the optional Android backup described below. Trackr requests **no permissions at all** — it has no internet access, so your data cannot be and is not sent to the developer or any third party. Trackr contains no analytics, no usage tracking, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Backup and device transfer

If you enable Android's backup or device-transfer features, Android may include Trackr's data (your log, photos, and settings) in **cloud backup to your own Google account** and in **direct transfer** when setting up a new device. These are Android operating-system features: the data goes to *your* Google account, not to the developer, and is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn Android backup off in your device settings. Trackr itself never accesses these backups.

## Sharing

If you choose to share a photo or other content out of Trackr using Android's share feature, that content goes to the app you pick. This happens only when you initiate it.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it — within the app, or by uninstalling Trackr (which removes its local data). If you use Android cloud backup, you can manage or delete backups through your Google account.

## Children

Trackr does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
