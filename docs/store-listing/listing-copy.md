## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Trackr — log anything, fast.

Trackr is a personal logger for whatever you want to keep track of. Create your own categories, then log an entry in as few as three taps — no account required, and your data stays on your device.

Built for speed
Tap the + button, pick a category, save. That's it. When you've already filtered to one category, Trackr skips the picker and it's even faster. The whole point is to capture the moment before you lose it.

Make it yours
Every category is yours to define — give it a name, an emoji, and a color. Then choose how it records a value:
• Occurrence — just mark that it happened
• Scale — rate it 1 to 10
• Yes / No
• Number with a unit (cups, km, mg — whatever fits)
• Free text — write whatever you want
• Duration — hours, minutes, seconds
• Exercise — sets and reps

Track habits, chores, caffeine, workouts, practice sessions, plants you watered — if you can name it, you can log it.

See your history
Your entries appear on a timeline, grouped by day, newest first. Tap a category chip to filter to just that one. Deleted something by accident? Swipe-to-delete comes with undo.

Add detail when you want it
Open any entry to edit its time, value, and notes, or attach photos — straight from the camera or your gallery.

Private by default
Trackr keeps your data on your device. There's no account to create — ever, not even to back it up — and it works fully offline, with your log in a private database on your phone. If you use Android's backup, your data goes to your own Google account — and nowhere you didn't choose. Your data is yours. If backup and sync ever arrive, they'll be optional — off unless you turn them on.

Free and open source. No tracking, no nonsense. A fast, private way to log the things you care about. Find the code — and file feedback or feature requests — at:
https://github.com/clahey/trackr
